# Code of Conduct

We want a safe, respectful and productive environment for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent and communicate clearly.
- Provide feedback focused on the work, not the person.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, hate speech, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Sharing private information without consent.

## Reporting

Report incidents to `security@zapzou.com.br`.
